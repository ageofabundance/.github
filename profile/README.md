# Age of Abundance

**Technology has made abundance possible. We believe everyone should benefit from it.**

Age of Abundance is an open-source software initiative dedicated to making powerful, useful applications freely available to everyone.

As artificial intelligence and modern development tools reduce the cost of creating software, we believe those savings should be passed on to the people who use it.

We build software without advertisements, mandatory subscriptions, or unnecessary paywalls.

Our projects are open source, community-driven, and designed to remain accessible.
Voluntary contributions and sustainable infrastructure help keep them running.

**Built for everyone. Free for everyone.**
